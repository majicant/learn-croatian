## Design Context

### Users
This is a personal Croatian reading and sentence-mining tool for a learner who wants to turn useful sentences into Anki cards with minimal interruption.

### Brand Personality
Calm, focused, practical. The interface should feel like a quiet study desk: fast, readable, and low-friction.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a restrained light interface with warm paper-like surfaces, strong reading typography, clear controls, and no decorative density. Prioritize speed and legibility over visual novelty.

### Design Principles
- Keep the default workflow to one sentence, one target, one action.
- Hide optional detail until it is needed.
- Prefer structured fields over freeform card editing.
- Make sync state visible without turning the app into an admin dashboard.
- Keep review-card content uncluttered and easy to parse in Anki.
